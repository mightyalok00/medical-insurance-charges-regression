# FAQ

## Why are three models used?
To compare a linear baseline, nonlinear polynomial effects, and tree-based interactions under the same evaluation framework.

## Which model is used in the dashboard?
The tuned Decision Tree pipeline is the primary dashboard prediction model, while the app also presents results from all three trained models.

## Is this suitable for real insurance decisions?
No. This is a portfolio and educational project and requires external validation and governance before any real operational use.
